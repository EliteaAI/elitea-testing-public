## Findings worth keeping

- **Batch-of-one costs ~2× per delivered case.** Measured, clean-boundary
  comparison of two non-overlapping eras:
  batched (08-01→08-04, 3 batches / 60 cases) = **$11.80/delivered**;
  single-case (08-04→08-06, 44 batches all caseCount=1) = **$22.10/delivered**
  (+87%; $/examined +66%). Cause: orchestration is paid per batch, so 47 lead
  sessions for 44 cases = $171.64 = $3.90/case = 22.2% of all spend.
  → Recommendation on record: batch when ≥3 approved cases are queued.
- **Blocked cases cost 1.85× the median delivered case** ($16.11 mean vs $9.41
  median). 9 blocked = $145.02 = 19% of window spend. Of that, $97.12 is honest
  red-for-a-real-product-bug; **$28.51 was lost to process/harness failures**
  (ELITEA-2292 $20.06 — reviewer left survivors unclassified twice;
  ELITEA-2437 $8.45 — subagent completed without calling StructuredOutput).
- **Rework is visible in sub-agent unit counts.** Clean case ≈ 1 QA + 1 impl unit
  ($3–7). Expensive tail runs 5–8 impl units (2336, 2338, 2304, 2352) — the fix
  rounds ARE the cost.
- **97.4% of every billed token is a cache re-read** (2.72B of 2.79B; output
  0.27%). Blended $0.277/M tokens, $103.21/M output tokens. Cost tracks
  context-size × turn-count, not thinking. The full `.agents/*` import in
  CLAUDE.md is the largest fixed per-turn cost — deliberate and load-bearing;
  flagged for measurement, NOT for blind trimming.

## Audit-method gotchas (recurrence tracking)

- `--resolved-from` **only scans one level** under `.agents/automation/` — missed
  `approved-next50/wave-*/report.json` again. **Recurrence 2** (first flagged in
  `2026-08-04-per-case.md`). Didn't distort this report (those waves' spend is
  out of window — verified), but silently under-counts sub-foldered campaigns.
- `reconciliation.externalOk: false` on a date-windowed run is a **known benign
  artifact** — hard-linked transcripts from prior audits still on disk inflate
  `ccusageMeteredSum`/`orphanFiles`. Confirm `costMethod: metered` (not
  `allocated`) and `internalOk: true`, then proceed. Third audit in a row.
- **Multi-day sessions land on their START date.** This is what made the era
  comparison clean: the 08-01 campaign session contributes $0 to a
  `--since 2026-08-03` window despite running into 08-04. Always verify the
  boundary (check the campaign session id is absent as `id` AND `parentId`)
  before claiming two windows are comparable.
