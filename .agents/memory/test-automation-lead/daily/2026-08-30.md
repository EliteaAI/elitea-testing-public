### The day's real lesson: canon beats dispatch notes

This morning I wrote wave 10's teardown catch into `.agents/testing.md` instead of into the wave's
dispatch prompt. This afternoon the reviewer applied that rule unprompted and blocked ELITEA-2416
for the same class of bug — a conversation id captured after the assertions that could orphan it.

The workflow has **no lead-notes arg**, and my first instinct was to smuggle the warning into case
titles. That would have worked once. Putting it in `.agents/testing.md` — which reaches every slot
through CLAUDE.md's `@`-import — works for every future wave and every other card. **When a lesson
is durable, spend the extra ten minutes to put it where slots read by default.**

### Closure-record grep trap (new, worth remembering)

14 testids reported `no` on BOTH refs and were in fact on `main`. They are runtime-composed —
`` data-testid={`${sectionTestId}-default-selector`} `` — which the closure record's bare-substring
stage 1 **cannot see at all**. `workflow.md` documents this exactly; I nearly wrote a false
"not promotable" row before reading its caveat. **Diff the component when a testid family reads
no/no but the tests demonstrably pass against it.** A testid that resolves live is on the branch
you're running; a grep saying otherwise is the grep being wrong.
