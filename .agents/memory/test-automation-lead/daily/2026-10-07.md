What actually worked: just call `ListAgents` again on the very next tool round (no sleep at all) — each round-trip's inherent latency was enough; the notification landed within two `ListAgents` checks (~1 min). Don't manufacture a wait — the harness delivers the notification on its own schedule as long as the turn stays open.
